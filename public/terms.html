In short

  • Assistant Helper is a free school project that helps teachers review students’ English writing.
  • Files are read inside your browser. The original files are never uploaded.
  • With a Google account, only your profile, your settings and the text and results of the work you analyze are saved to your private space in the cloud. Nobody else can see them.

1. About the service

Assistant Helper is a web application created by Team 7 (Equipo 7) as a school project for the Bilingual Executive Secretary program. It checks similarity between submissions loaded in the same session, document format, writing quality and heuristic AI indicators. It is offered free of charge, “as is”, without warranties of any kind, and it may change or stop being available at any time.

By pressing Continue with Google, Use without an account, Start session or I agree you confirm that you have read and accept these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

2. Acceptable use

  • Use Assistant Helper only for teaching and reviewing schoolwork.
  • Only load documents you are allowed to review, such as work your students submitted to you.
  • Do not upload content that is illegal, or try to access other people’s accounts or data.
  • Do not try to break, overload or misuse the application or the services it relies on.
  • You are responsible for keeping your Google account secure and for the decisions you make with the results.

3. Results and limitations

  • Similarity is calculated only against the files loaded in your session. The application never searches the internet.
  • AI indicators are statistical signals, not proof. They can be wrong, especially with short texts, and must never be the only reason for a grade or a sanction.
  • Text recognition (OCR) of scanned pages and images can make mistakes with handwriting or blurry photos.
  • Always review the original document before making a decision about a student.

4. Google account data

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose it, Assistant Helper receives from Google:

Data Why it is used
Your name and profile photo To greet you and show who is signed in. Your name also appears on the PDF reports you export.
Your email address To identify your account and suggest the same account when you open Google Drive.
A unique account ID To keep your saved work separate from everyone else’s.

Sign-in is handled by Google through Firebase Authentication. Assistant Helper never sees or stores your Google password.

5. Google Drive access

When you press Choose from Drive, Google asks for permission to use the drive.file scope. This permission only lets Assistant Helper open the files you select in Google’s own file picker (and files you upload through it).

  • The application cannot list, read, change or delete any other file in your Drive.
  • The selected files are downloaded directly from Google to your browser to be read and analyzed. They do not pass through any server of ours.
  • Google Docs are converted to Word format (.docx) by Google during the download.
  • The access token is kept only in the memory of the open tab, expires after about one hour and is discarded when you close the tab.
  • The application never modifies, moves, shares or deletes your Drive files.

6. What is stored and where

Without an account

Nothing is stored. Everything stays in the memory of the browser tab and is removed when you close or reload it.

With a Google account

The following is saved in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), in a private space that only your account can read or write:

  • Your profile (name, role, group) and your review settings.
  • For each analyzed submission: file name, student name, group, assignment title, extracted text, analysis results, notes, review decision and final comment.

Never stored: the original files (Word, PDF or images), images inside documents, your Google password and your Google Drive access token.

Firestore is operated by Google and protected with security rules and encrypted connections.

7. Sharing and Limited Use

Assistant Helper does not sell, rent or share your information or your students’ information with anyone. It does not show advertising and does not use your data to train artificial intelligence models. No person on the project team reads your saved data.

Assistant Helper’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Your choices and deletion

  • Remove a submission from the list to delete it from your account.
  • Clear all submissions (sidebar) deletes every saved submission and keeps your profile and settings.
  • Settings → Privacy and terms → Delete my saved data deletes your profile, settings and all submissions from the cloud and signs you out.
  • You can remove Assistant Helper’s access to your Google account and Drive at any time from your Google account connections.

9. Student information

Submissions can contain personal information about students. As the teacher, you decide what to load and you must follow your school’s rules and the applicable data protection laws. Load only what is needed for the review and delete it when it is no longer necessary.

10. Changes and contact

If these terms change in an important way, the application will ask you to accept the new version before you continue. Questions about these terms or about your data can be sent to Team 7 (Equipo 7) through the school.
